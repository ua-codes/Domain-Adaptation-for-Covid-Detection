### Accessing the Data:
- For reproduction, the data must be placed in a Google Drive folder named `COVID_Project`.
- The notebook `notebooks/COVID19_Final_Model.ipynb` is configured to mount Google Drive and look for paths in `/content/drive/MyDrive/COVID_Project/`.
